Party Party Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Party Party is an offline party game app for groups of friends. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Party Party has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no backend server. The app does not request internet access and does not send any data off your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences on your phone, the player names you type in, the visual style you picked, and your sound and vibration settings, so they are ready next time. This data never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Device sensors

The Charades game reads the motion sensor (accelerometer) so you can tilt the phone to mark a card correct or pass. Sensor readings are used on the spot and are not stored or shared.

Children

Party Party is a party game for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not have any. Apple's App Store and Google Play may collect information about your download under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google).

Play nice

Party Party is for entertainment. Your group picks the penalties, so keep them safe and fun, and anyone can skip a dare at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.

Sip Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Sip Quest is an offline party game app for adults of legal drinking age. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Sip Quest has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no backend server. The app does not request internet access and does not send any data off your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences on your phone, the player names you type in, the visual style you picked, and your sound and vibration settings, so they are ready next time. This data never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Device sensors

The Charades game reads the motion sensor (accelerometer) so you can tilt the phone to mark a card correct or pass. Sensor readings are used on the spot and are not stored or shared.

Children

Sip Quest is intended only for adults of legal drinking age in their country and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not have any. Google Play may collect information about your download under Google's own privacy policy.

Drink responsibly

Sip Quest is for entertainment. Know your limits, never drink and drive, and any player can swap a drink for water at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
